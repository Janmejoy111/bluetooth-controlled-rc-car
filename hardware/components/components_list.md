\# 🔩 Components List – Bluetooth Controlled RC Car



Below is the list of all hardware components used in this project.



\## 🧠 Microcontroller

\- Arduino Nano (ATmega328P)



\## 📡 Communication Module

\- HC-05 Bluetooth Module



\## ⚙️ Motor Driver

\- L298N Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver Module



\## 🚗 Motors \& Chassis

\- DC BO Gear Motors (2x)

\- RC Car Chassis

\- Rubber Wheels (2x)



\## 🔋 Power Supply

\- 18650 Lithium-Ion Batteries (2x)

\- Battery Holder

\- Power Switch



\## 🔌 Connecting Components

\- Jumper Wires (Male-to-Male, Male-to-Female)

\- Breadboard



\## 🛠️ Miscellaneous

\- Screws and Nuts

\- Insulating Tape
